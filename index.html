About Me

When I was 18 and on the cusp of going off to school to study Bioengineering, I had a pivotal moment of realization that I would be leaving one whole half of my skillset fully untapped if I didn't at least try to get into design school, so I did: I applied to one of the top schools in India, did a great job on all the on-paper tests and in the interview, and missed out on the cutoff by a few points because I completely fumbled the 3D craft work test. I decided that I would go to Engineering school and do design on the side on my own time anyway.

It has been a decade since then, and I've worked primarily in digital health and wellness, leveraging my eye for design to bring the customer's vision to life. My technical background helps me translate and break the problem down to the team, and then I create a tailor-made solution that addresses the pain points.

I especially love accessible design, so transforming my work across different platforms, for a wide range of user demographics, with gamified elements to increase engagement is my favorite aspect of being a Product Designer.

At an Autumn Moon Viewing Ceremony

I am from Chennai, a coastal city in South India, and I wanted to capture my ethnicity through this portfolio – the dot motif splashed across this website is a traditional Tamil artwork called Kolam (கோலம்) that is typically drawn in rice flour on the floor at the front door to the house. The Kolam is said to symbolize and bring prosperity to the home, and sometimes the geometrical pattern is enhanced by a red brick/limestone powder dye. I took inspiration from this practice to create this website, and the colors and fonts are derived from my cultural background.

I currently work on the Product Team of a digital wellness tech company, and I live in Champaign, Illinois.
